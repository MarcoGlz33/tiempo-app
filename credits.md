# Tiempo Credits and Licenses

Most of what you see and hear in Tiempo was made for it. Here are the pieces others shared, with thanks.

## Recordings

Four soundscapes play recordings shared on [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain):

- **Rain on a window:** [“rain against window 2”](https://freesound.org/people/bastipictures/sounds/243781/) by bastipictures.
- **Rain & distant thunder:** the same rain, with
  [“Light Rain Thunder From Window”](https://freesound.org/people/chrisw92/sounds/159684/) by chrisw92.
- **Ocean waves:** [“Atlantic Ocean, Ocean Waves Ambience sound.mp3”](https://freesound.org/people/INNORECORDS/sounds/457740/)
  by INNORECORDS.
- **Crackling fire:** [“Fireplace Fire Crackling”](https://freesound.org/people/RyanKingArt/sounds/717579/) by RyanKingArt.

Everything else you hear was synthesized for Tiempo: the noises, Garden, Zen, the completion sound Patter and the gong.

## Photograph

Water's reflections use [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), a studio photograph by Sergej Majboroda
from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Open-source code

Two parts of Tiempo adapt code shared under the MIT License. Their notices, below, also ship inside the apps.

- **Notifications:** Tiempo's notification scheduling is adapted from [Dawnly](https://github.com/theakhinabraham/dawnly)
  by Akhin Abraham.
- **Water's waves:** the wave simulation is adapted from [threejs-water](https://github.com/jeantimex/threejs-water) by
  Yong Su, a port of Evan Wallace's [WebGL Water](https://madebyevan.com/webgl-water/).
